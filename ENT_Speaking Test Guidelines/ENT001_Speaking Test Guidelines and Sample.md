# ENT001 - Speaking Test Guidelines and Sample

**Trường Đại học FPT / FPT University**

---

## 1. General Overview (Tổng quan kỳ thi)

- **Hình thức thi:** Thí sinh thi cá nhân (Students take the test individually).
- **Chọn chủ đề:** Sinh viên chọn chủ đề ngẫu nhiên (sử dụng công cụ bốc thăm, ví dụ: https://wheelofnames.com/ ).
- **Cấu trúc bài thi gồm 2 phần:**
  - **Part 1: Introduction** — Giám khảo hỏi thí sinh 5 câu hỏi từ danh sách quy định. (*Thời lượng: 2 phút*).
  - **Part 2: Picture description** — Thí sinh miêu tả một bức tranh dựa trên các câu hỏi gợi ý (*2 phút chuẩn bị, 2 - 3 phút nói*).

---

## 2. Instructions for Examiner (Hướng dẫn dành cho Giám khảo)

### Starting the test (Bắt đầu bài thi)
> *"Good morning/afternoon. Welcome to the speaking test. There are two parts to this test. In the first part, I will ask you 5 questions. In Part 2, you will describe a picture based on some questions I will give you."*

### Part 1: Introduction
1. **Giới thiệu Part 1:**
   > *"Let’s begin with Part 1. I’m going to ask you 5 questions."*
2. **Chuyển sang Part 2:**
   > *"That’s the end of Part 1. Let’s move on to Part 2."*

### Part 2: Picture description
> *"Now, for part 2, I’m going to show you a picture and you need to describe it in 2 to 3 minutes. You will have 2 minutes to prepare. I will also give you some questions to help you think about what to say."*

### Ending the test (Kết thúc bài thi)
> *"That is the end of the speaking test. Thank you."*

---

## 3. Speaking Test Sample (Đề thi mẫu)

**Chủ đề (Topic):** Activities

### Part 1: Introduction (2 - 3 minutes)
> **Note to the examiner:**  
> Do not give the student time to prepare. Ask the student to introduce himself. Additionally, remind the student to answer using complete sentences.

**Danh sách 5 câu hỏi phỏng vấn:**
1. Where are you from?
2. What are your favorite clothes to school?
3. What time do you eat breakfast?
4. What is your favorite leisure activity?
5. What's your typical day like?

---

### Part 2: Picture description (2-min preparation, 2-min answer)
> **Note to the examiner:**  
> Give the student 2 minutes to prepare his answer for this part. The student has a maximum of 2 minutes to talk about the pictures using the given questions as guides.

**Hình ảnh mô tả:**  
Một người mẹ đang vui đùa giặt quần áo cùng em bé ngồi trong rổ đồ trước máy giặt cửa ngang (Mẹ mặc áo trắng, quần jeans rách; em bé quấn khăn trắng trên đầu).

**4 câu hỏi định hướng (Guiding questions):**
1. Who are they?
2. Where are they?
3. What are they wearing?
4. What are they doing?
